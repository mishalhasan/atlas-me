# AtlasMe: A Visual Travel Mapping App

---

## 🧭 Project Overview

AtlasMe is a simple travel tracker that helps you organize where you’ve been and where you want to go—without the clutter of bookmarks, notes, or scattered saved posts.

It’s designed for people who enjoy planning travel and collecting places, but want a hassle-free way to keep everything in one place. Your visited countries and planned destinations become a clear, visual list that’s easy to maintain and satisfying to grow over time.

AtlasMe also makes it easy to share your travel goals and progress with friends or other travel enthusiasts.

With AtlasMe, you can:

- Track countries and places you’ve visited
- Maintain a clear bucket list of destinations you want to go to
- Visually see your travel progress over time
- Share your travel profile with friends or fellow travelers

AtlasMe is built for clarity, motivation, and the simple satisfaction of watching your travel list grow.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Travel inspiration is usually scattered across videos, bookmarks, screenshots, and saved posts, making it difficult to maintain a clear and actionable list of places to visit. At the same time, keeping track of visited countries and destinations often lacks structure and consistency.

There is a need for a lightweight, centralized travel tracker that turns this fragmented intent into a simple, organized system. AtlasMe solves this by combining visited places and travel aspirations into one clean, shareable view.
